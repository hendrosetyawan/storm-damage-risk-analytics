COSMOS

Texas homeowners insurance · 254 counties

Two counties can report the same storm damage and still be very different risks for an insurer. One loses a little every spring; the other loses almost everything on one afternoon. Here every county is a glowing storm: its shape is the peril that drives its damage, its size is how much damage it has, and its colour is what that means for homeowners insurance.

How to read the cosmos

Haila cluster of ice stones: hail causes most of the county's damage
Thunderstorm winda spinning storm spiral: straight-line wind causes most damage
Tornadoa funnel: tornadoes cause most damage
  • Money: dollar amounts (NOAA storm damage, TDI paid losses) are a stack of paper money under each storm: taller stack, more dollars; each strapped bundle is the same amount. Homeowners policies are shown by storm size instead.
  • Colour: the insurance lens chosen in the top tabs, from cool (low) to hot (high).
  • Map places counties where they are, Pattern groups them by recurring vs. catastrophic damage, Ladder ranks them on the colour measure.
  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, hover for numbers, click a county for its insurance card and worst storm days.
