How to read the 3D story

Hailice stones: hail causes most of the county's damage
Thunderstorm winda storm spiral: straight-line wind causes most damage
Tornadoa funnel: tornadoes cause most damage
  • Each 3D storm is one Texas county.
  • Stacks under a storm show amounts: green money for dollars, red bills for claims paid, blue policy paper for insured homes, rose sheets for homes without a policy. Taller = more; every bundle is the same amount.
  • Colour is named at the bottom of the screen. Each chapter rings and labels two counties that make its point by contrast.
  • Back / Next or the arrow keys move through the chapters. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, hover for numbers, click a county for its insurance card.
