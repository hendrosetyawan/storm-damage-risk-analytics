How to read the 3D story

Hailice stones: hail causes most of the county's damage
Thunderstorm winda storm spiral: straight-line wind causes most damage
Tornadoa funnel: tornadoes cause most damage
  • Each 3D storm is one Texas county.
  • Stacks under a storm show amounts: green money for dollars, red bills for claims paid, blue policy paper for insured homes, rose sheets for homes without a policy. Taller = more; every bundle is the same amount.
  • Colour is named at the bottom of the screen. Each chapter rings and labels two counties that make its point by contrast.
  • Story walks through seven chapters with Back / Next or the arrow keys; Explore (top right, or after the last chapter) lets you pick any colour, layout and stack.
  • Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, hover for numbers. Click a county to open its own 3D view: its hail, thunderstorm wind and tornado above it, its homes as houses and its money as paper stacks; Back to Texas or Esc returns. Its storm calendar (year × month) shows when storms came; click a month to see only its reports.
  • Find (top right, or press /) jumps to any county or chapter. In Explore, the Years chart picks one year or a range, or plays the 20 years in turn.
